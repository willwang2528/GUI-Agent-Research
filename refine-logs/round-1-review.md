## Strongest Combined Rejection

The study could have returned a positive result merely because its measurement ontology already represented trajectories as proposition → dependency → repair obligations, while the candidate generator itself had no recall audit. At the same time, Step 1 used causal phrases such as “old state governed the action,” even though published traces only show an external action compatible with `p_old` and incompatible with the normative action under `p_new`. A positive observational audit would therefore not identify Memory, and a reconstructed replay without system/environment identity would not explain the original hosted Agent.
